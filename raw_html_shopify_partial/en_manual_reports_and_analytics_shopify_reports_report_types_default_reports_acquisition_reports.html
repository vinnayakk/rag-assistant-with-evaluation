Acquisition reports

Acquisition reports display information about how visitors come to your store. The following acquisition reports are available:

  • Sessions by referrer
  • Sessions by location
  • Sessions over time
  • Visitors over time

Note

The Shopify Analytics session measurement rollout runs from September 21 to 23, 2026. When you compare reports across the rollout period, treat the update as a measurement change for sessions, conversion rate, and other session-based metrics. Learn more about Changes to sessions and conversion rate in Shopify Analytics.

When you open an acquisition report, the data is up to date, give or take a few seconds. You can reopen or refresh the report to include newer data.

Note

Reports that include today in their time range might have minor changes in session counts as the current day progresses. Because a visitor's session duration extends for every action performed, a session can last for multiple hours depending on the visitor's behavior. This means that a completed session can occur much later in the day for a session beginning earlier in the day, and can change the total number of completed sessions for the timeframe including when the session began.

Acquisition reports are different from the Total sales by referrer report because acquisition reports do not show converted sales or the amount of orders—just visitors to your website. These reports apply to the Online Store sales channel only.

Understanding acquisition data

The acquisition data in your reports helps you understand how visitors find and interact with your online store. The following sections explain how acquisition data is collected and what it means, so you can make informed decisions about your marketing efforts and website performance.

Sidekick

You can ask Sidekick about your acquisition data, including sessions by location, sessions by referrer, sessions over time, and visitors over time. Use a prompt such as Show me sessions by referrer for the last 30 days.

Note

Historical data for sessions-based metrics go back only to October 1, 2022. Data prior to that date isn't available for sessions-based metrics. The following metrics have been impacted:

  • Add to cart rate
  • Bounce rate
  • Bounces
  • Checkout conversion rate
  • Completed checkout rate
  • Conversion rate (online store)
  • Online store visitors
  • Pageviews
  • Pageviews per session
  • Reached checkout rate
  • Session duration
  • Sessions
  • Sessions that completed checkout
  • Sessions that reached checkout
  • Sessions with cart additions

Tip

You can filter your data by human and bot traffic. Learn more about bot filtering in Shopify analytics and reports.

Types of referrer dimensions

Dimensions such as Referrer path or UTM-related dimensions display raw data based on the session. However, you can also group your session data by differing levels of specificity based on marketing channel attributes. The values for these dimensions are processed and standardized to help give higher-quality insights into your acquisition data:

  • Referring channel: The specific source by which a visitor arrived at your online store. Examples: Instagram, Google, or Facebook.
  • Referring platform: The platform company by which a visitor arrived at your online store. Examples: Meta, Google, or Microsoft.
  • Referring medium: The type of referring channel that led to the session. Examples: social, search, or email.
  • Traffic type: The category of traffic that led to the session. Examples: direct, organic, or paid.

Comparing sessions and visitors

The number of sessions and the number of visitors are based on cookies. Cookies are small files that are stored on a visitor's device, such as a desktop or smartphone, when they visit your online store. One cookie identifies the device (the visitor). Another cookie keeps track of the length of the session.

A session ends after 30 minutes of inactivity. Because the same visitor can have multiple sessions, the number of sessions is usually higher than the number of visitors.

Example: A user views products on your store for 20 minutes, then returns to your store 2 hours later for another 10 minutes. Two sessions and one visitor are reported. If the user instead views your store for 5 minutes, leaves, and returns 10 minutes later, then one session and one visitor are reported.

If you want to view your online store and not have it count as a session in your acquisition reports, then on your desktop Shopify admin, click View store to open your store in a new browser tab. Any other method of viewing your online store on desktop counts as a session in your reports.

If you view your online store from the Shopify app on iPhone or Android, then it counts as a session in your acquisition reports.

View your acquisition reports

You can filter your Reports list to display only Acquisition reports.

Steps:

Desktop

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Analytics > Reports.

  2. Click the Category filter.

  3. Click Acquisition to filter the reports to display only Acquisition reports.

Mobile

  1. From the Shopify app, tap the icon.

  2. Tap Analytics > Reports.

  3. Tap the filter icon, and then tap Category.

  4. Tap Acquisition to filter the reports to display only Acquisition reports.

  5. Tap < Filter by or ←, and then X to navigate back to the filtered Reports list.

Sessions by location

The Sessions by location report displays the countries, regions, and cities from which visitors are accessing your website.

Sessions by referrer

The Sessions by referrer report displays whether your visitors are coming to your store directly, from search, or by a referral.

You can see similar information on your Analytics overview dashboard, but the Sessions by referrer report gives you more detailed insights.

This report covers sessions only. To compare your organic and referring channels against your paid channels, with sales and ad spend included, refer to Performance by marketing channel.

Sessions over time

The Sessions over time report displays the number of visitors and sessions for the time range.

You can select a unit of time in the Group by drop-down menu to control how the data is grouped.

You can compare data from different date ranges in this report.

This report is similar to the Visitors over time report, but Sessions is selected as the primary metric.

Visitors over time

The Visitors over time report displays the number of unique visitors to the online store over time. By default, the time period displayed is the last 7 days, and compares it to the data from the previous 7 days.

This report is similar to the Sessions over time report, but Online store visitors is selected as the primary metric.
