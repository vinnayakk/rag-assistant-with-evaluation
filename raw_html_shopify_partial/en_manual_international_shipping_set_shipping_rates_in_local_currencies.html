Set up shipping rates in local currencies

Note

Shipping rates in local currencies are only possible when configuring fixed rates. If you use carrier calculated rates with DHL Express, then these rates are automatically converted to your store's base currency.

When setting up fixed shipping rates in an international market, you can choose to use your store’s base currency or the local currency for that market. If you select the local option, then the checkout will display shipping rates in your customer's local currency.

For example, you're a merchant with a primary market of Canada and have a large European market that you ship to. You can choose to set up and advertise localized shipping rates for the European market such as €15 flat rate shipping, or free shipping with a minimum €200 spend. For customers in the European market, the checkout displays those advertised shipping rates in their local currency.

Note

Only stores using Shopify Payments can use this feature. Refer to the list of supported countries to find out if Shopify Payments is available to you.

If you sell in local currencies, then you need to have a country selector for customers to select their local country or region so that your store displays their local currency.

The currency that you choose will be applied to the product price and the cart-value conditions that you’ve set up.

Caution

Pre-existing rates won't automatically change after you add shipping rates in local currencies. Existing rates must be deleted if you want to only display shipping rates in the local currency.

To add fixed shipping rates in local currencies, either of the following must be true:

  • Shipping zone must contain only a single-country.
  • Alternatively, if multiple countries are in one shipping zone, then they must all share the same currency.

Preview conversions

You can preview the results of any conversion applied to your fixed shipping rates.

Steps:

  1. From your Shopify admin, go to Markets.

  2. Click the market that you want to preview.

  3. Click Shipping.

  4. Click the country or region to display which shipping rates currently apply.

  5. Click Manage in shipping to preview both the local rate and the converted amount in your store's base currency.

Note

When setting shipping rates for large zones containing countries that use many different currencies, automatic conversion from your shop’s currency is the only option.

You might opt to continue to set shipping rates in your store’s base currency (not localized). These rates will be automatically converted to cover your costs.
