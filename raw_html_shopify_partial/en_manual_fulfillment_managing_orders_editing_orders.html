Editing orders

You can edit orders after they're placed to make adjustments before fulfillment. Order editing lets you respond to customer requests, fix mistakes, or update order details without canceling and recreating the order.

When you edit an order, you can add or remove products, adjust item quantities, and update shipping fees. You can also apply manual discounts to specific items or remove existing discounts. After you save your changes, if additional payment is required, then you can send the customer an updated invoice. If the order total decreases, then you can issue a refund.

Before you edit an order, review the considerations for editing orders to understand how changes affect payments, fulfillment, and reporting.

Sidekick

You can ask Sidekick to edit an order: add or remove products, change quantities, or apply a discount. Sidekick makes the changes in the order editor for you to review. Learn more about editing orders with Sidekick.
