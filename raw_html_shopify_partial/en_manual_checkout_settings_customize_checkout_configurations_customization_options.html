Customization options for Thank you and Order status pages

You can customize your Thank you and Order status pages with built-in Shopify features, apps, and custom apps. Use this reference to compare the options that are available for common customization categories, such as tracking, localization, returns, shipping, and styling.

Most customizations are managed from the checkout and accounts editor. App support depends on the type of customization that you want to add, such as app blocks for page content or app pixels for tracking and analytics.

Choosing a customization method

The best customization method depends on what you want to change and how much control you need:

  • Use Shopify features when a setting or built-in feature already supports the experience that you want to offer.
  • Use apps when you want to add blocks, tracking, or other features without building a custom solution. You can search the Shopify App Store for apps that provide the type of customization that you need. Learn more about customizing your checkout and customer accounts with apps.
  • Use custom apps when you need a custom experience that isn't available in Shopify or through an app. Work with your developer or hire a Shopify Partner to build custom functionality.

For app blocks, you can confirm which pages a block supports after you install the app. In the checkout and accounts editor, go to the Apps tab, and then click + next to a block to review which pages the block can be added to. For tracking and analytics, apps that provide web pixels are compatible with the Thank you page.

Accessibility

The Thank you and Order status pages and blocks are designed to be accessible and compliant with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) and the Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT). Accessibility updates are applied automatically. To learn more, refer to the Shopify Accessibility Statement.

Authentication

Customers can view their Order status page without signing in for a certain amount of time. After a certain amount of time, the link expires, and a redacted view displays. The authentication options for your customer to view their order details after this time depend on which version of customer accounts you use:

  • If you're using the new version of customer accounts, then customers can either sign in to view their order details, or send a new link to the email or phone number they used at checkout.
  • If you use legacy accounts or have sign-in links turned off, then customers can only send a new link to the email or phone number they used at checkout.

Customers might also be prompted to sign in on the Order status page in the following cases:

  • A block that you've added to your Order status page requires authentication
  • The customer navigates to another customer account page from the Order status page, when you're using the new version of customer accounts

You can also connect your own identity provider to customer accounts.

Bundles

Bundles customization options
Customization optionDescription
Shopify featuresShopify Bundles is a free Shopify-built app that lets you create and edit fixed product bundles and multipacks from your Shopify admin. Learn more about product bundles.
Shopify App StoreSearch the Shopify App Store for bundle, upsell, or cross-sell apps.
Custom appsDevelopers can use the Cart Transform API to customize the pricing and merchandising of cart line items, which apply to the Thank you and Order status pages. This includes the following types of customizations:
  • Expand a single cart line to form a bundle of components or add-on products
  • Merge multiple cart lines into a single line that represents a bundle
  • Update line items to override a product price, title, or image

Note

The Thank you and Order status pages don't support hiding individual items within a bundle.

Cross-border

Use Shopify's international sales tools to help you manage and expand your global sales.

Customizations per market aren't supported on the Thank you and Order status pages.

Navigation

Customizations for Order status page, Thank you page, and footer navigation
Customization optionDescription
Order status page navigation

The Order status page is a customer account page. If you're using the new version of customer accounts, then navigation to customer account pages displays in your store header in the customer account menu.

If you're still using legacy customer accounts, or you've hidden customer accounts, then header navigation doesn't display on the Order status page.

Thank you page navigationYou can use apps such as the Shopify Checkout Blocks app to customize the header and footer of the Thank you page with text, links, or images. Header customizations display under your shop name, and footer customizations display under the footer navigation of the page.
Footer navigationWhen you add store policies, they automatically display as footer links on the Thank you and Order status pages.
