Resetting passwords

You can reset your login password if you forget it, or if you want to keep your account more secure by resetting it. If you have two-step authentication activated, then you'll need to verify your account. You can also update your password at any time from your Shopify account.

Your password must be at least 8 characters, and it can't begin or end with a space.

Resetting your password removes any third-party login services, such as Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple. You can still sign in using your email address and new password, at which point you can add your third-party login service again.

If you sign in with Security Assertion Markup Language (SAML) single sign-on (SSO), then you can't reset your password through the Shopify login page. Your organization's identity provider manages your password. Contact your organization's administrator for help with SAML login.

If you created your account with a third-party login service and never set a password, then the login page might not offer Forgot password?. If you can't log in or complete a verification prompt using an available method, then contact Shopify Support.

If you forgot your Shopify POS PIN, then you can change your PIN.

Reset your password when you're logged out of your account

If you forgot your password and are logged out of your account, then you can reset your password. You'll need access to the email address on your Shopify account and any additional security measures, such as two-step-authentication, to reset your password.

Steps:

Desktop
  1. From the Shopify login page, enter your store address and click Next.
  2. Enter the email that you used to create your Shopify account, and then click Continue with email.
  3. Click Forgot password?.
  4. Enter the email that you used to create your Shopify account.
  5. Click Reset password.
  6. You're sent an email with a link to reset your password. In the email, click Reset password.
  7. Enter your new password in the Confirm password field.
  8. Click Reset password.
  9. Return to your Shopify login page and log in with your new password.
Mobile
  1. Open the Shopify app, and then tap Log in.
  2. Enter the email that you used to create your Shopify account.
  3. Tap Continue with email.
  4. Tap Forgot password?. If you can't access the email address, then change to your secondary email address.
  5. Tap Email password reset.
  6. You're sent an email with a link to reset your password. In the email, tap Reset password.
  7. The password-reset screen opens in your device's browser. Enter a new password.

Caution

Each of your passwords should be unique. Don't use the same password for more than one account, even if the accounts are related.

  1. Enter your new password again in the Confirm password field.
  2. Tap Reset password.
  3. Return to the Shopify app and log in with your new password.

Reset your password when you're logged in to your account

You can reset your password at any time from your Shopify account's Security page. If you have two-step authentication active, then you might need to perform additional verification.

Early access

A refreshed design for the Shopify admin is rolling out gradually, so your store name might be in a different location than this article describes. The refreshed design changes only the appearance and layout of the Shopify admin.

Steps:

Desktop

  1. From your Shopify admin, click your store name.

  2. Click your profile, and then click Security.

  3. In the Password section, tap Change your password.

  4. In the Change your password dialog, enter your current password, and then enter a new password, and the new password again to confirm.

  5. Tap Change password.

Mobile
  1. From your Shopify app, tap the icon next to your store name.
  2. Tap your name.
  3. Tap Manage account. It opens in your mobile browser.
  4. Tap the Security tab.
  5. In the Password section, tap Change your password.
  6. In the Change your password dialog, enter your current password, and then enter a new password, and the new password again to confirm.
  7. Tap Change password.

Reset your password to get past a verification prompt

If an unavailable passkey blocks your login, then follow the steps in this section. If you're signed in and a verification prompt blocks a sensitive action, then first try the options in troubleshooting verification prompts. Use this section when those options don't resolve the prompt. Your account needs a password to reset it through Forgot password?, and this procedure doesn't apply to accounts that use SAML single sign-on.

Resetting your password removes every passkey on your account, including working passkeys on other devices. It also removes third-party login services such as Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple. You can add these methods again after you log in. You need access to your account email address to complete the reset.

A password reset doesn't remove two-step authentication or the secure sign-in requirement. If you've also lost access to two-step authentication, then refer to troubleshooting two-step authentication code issues instead.

Steps:

  1. From the Shopify login page, enter your email address, and then click Continue with email.
  2. If a passkey or another login method is offered instead of a password, then click Log in using a different method, and then select the password option.
  3. Click Forgot password? instead of entering your password. If the password option or Forgot password? isn't available, then contact Shopify Support instead.
  4. Click Email password reset.
  5. In the reset email, click Reset password, and then follow the instructions to set a new password.
  6. Log in with your new password. Complete any two-step authentication prompt using a working method or a recovery code.
  7. If you're prompted to set up a passkey or two-step authentication, then complete the setup.
  8. If you were completing a sensitive action, then return to it and follow any verification prompts.

If the password reset doesn't resolve the prompt, then refer to troubleshoot verification prompts in the Shopify admin for other options.

Note

If you think your account has been hacked or compromised, then contact Shopify Support immediately. Use the steps below only if you've lost access to your email and don't think someone else has accessed your account.

Reset your password when you can't access your email

If you need to update an email address that's associated with your Shopify account but you aren't able to do so yourself, then you can confirm your identity with Shopify and submit a request to reset your account email and password.

Steps:

I have logged in within the last 3 months

  1. Go to accounts.shopify.com and select Your store.

  2. Enter your current login credentials.

  3. On the password page, click Forgot password.

  4. Click Recover account.

  5. Provide a new email address for account ownership, and then click Continue.

  6. Enter the 6-digit verification code sent to your new email address, and then click Verify.

  7. If additional information is needed, then follow the instructions to verify your identity using Stripe's verification portal. Learn more about identity verification on Shopify.

After submitting the required information, you receive a confirmation email to let you know the account recovery process has begun. When the account recovery process is complete, you receive an additional email with further steps to help you regain access to your account.

I have not logged in within the last 3 months

  1. Go to accounts.shopify.com and select Your store.

  2. Enter your current login credentials.

  3. On the password page, click Forgot password.

  4. On the Reactivate your account page, click Lost access to email?.

  5. Provide a new email address for account ownership, and then click Continue.

  6. Enter the 10-digit verification code sent to your new email address, then click Verify.

  7. If additional information is needed, then follow the instructions to verify your identity using Stripe's verification portal. Learn more about identity verification on Shopify.

After submitting the required information, you receive a confirmation email to let you know the account recovery process has begun. When the account recovery process is complete, you receive an additional email with further steps to help you regain access to your account.
