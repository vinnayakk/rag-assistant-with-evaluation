Building and managing your store with third-party AI tools

You can connect your Shopify store to a third-party AI tool such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or Muse, and then manage your store from inside that tool. Some AI tools and vibe code partners can also create a Shopify store for you.

This section covers AI tools that you connect to a store you own. To make your products discoverable to shoppers who use AI assistants instead, refer to Shopify agentic storefronts, Selling on ChatGPT, and the Agentic plan.

Managing your store from an AI tool

Connect ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or Muse to your store. To install an integration and set how much data it can access, refer to authorizing AI tool access to your store. To have an AI tool point you to the right integration, refer to Shopify skill.md for AI tools.

Reviewing what each AI tool can do

The Shopify plugin for ChatGPT and the Shopify connector for Claude can build a store from a storefront preview, manage a store that you own, and source products from Shopify Collective suppliers for you to import. The Shopify connector for Muse can connect an existing store or send you through Shopify signup to create and link a new store. The Shopify connector for Perplexity manages an existing store only.

Creating a store with an AI tool or vibe code partner

If an AI tool or a vibe code partner created your store, then claim it and finish setup. Refer to Setting up a store created with an AI tool or vibe code partner and Building a store with a vibe code partner. To create a store from the command line instead, refer to Creating a Shopify store with the Stripe Projects CLI.

Connecting an AI tool from your local device

If you're a developer connecting from your own machine, then refer to the Shopify CLI connector.

Reviewing access and resolving issues

Review what data is shared, what you're responsible for, and what a connected tool can change. Refer to considerations for using third-party AI tools with your Shopify store.
